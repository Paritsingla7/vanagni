Next-day forest-fire risk for Uttarakhand

For every 5 km grid cell in Uttarakhand, this model predicts whether a satellite will detect a forest fire there tomorrow, from the cell's fire history, the weather, its fuel and terrain, and how close it is to people — roads, settlements, cropland.

The question it tests: in a fire regime that is 90–95% human-caused, do human-proximity features add more predictive lift than weather? Confirmed or refuted, both are a real result.
